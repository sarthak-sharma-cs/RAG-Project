TBHSI Employee Handbook

Version: 3.2
Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Organization: TBHSI Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

Introduction

Welcome to TBHSI Technologies Pvt. Ltd. This handbook outlines the key employment policies applicable to all full-time employees of the company. These policies are designed to ensure fairness, transparency, employee well-being, and compliance with applicable labor laws.

Employees are expected to familiarize themselves with the contents of this handbook and seek clarification from the Human Resources (HR) department whenever required.

1. Annual Leave Policy
Purpose

The Annual Leave Policy enables employees to take time off for personal rest, vacations, family commitments, and other non-work-related activities.

Eligibility

All permanent employees are eligible for annual leave upon successful completion of probation.

Leave Entitlement

Employees are entitled to:

24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave is credited monthly at the rate of 2 days per month.
Part-time employees receive leave on a pro-rated basis.
Leave Application Process

Employees must:

Submit leave requests through the HRMS portal.
Obtain approval from their reporting manager.
Apply at least 7 calendar days in advance for planned leave exceeding 3 consecutive working days.
Carry Forward
Employees may carry forward up to 10 unused annual leave days into the following year.
Any balance above 10 days will automatically lapse unless special approval is granted by HR.
Leave Encashment

Employees may encash unused annual leave during separation from the company, subject to local regulations and company guidelines.

Blackout Periods

Certain business-critical periods may be designated as blackout periods where annual leave approvals may be restricted.

2. Maternity Leave Policy
Purpose

TBHSI is committed to supporting employees during pregnancy, childbirth, adoption, and early childcare responsibilities.

Eligibility

This policy applies to:

Full-time female employees.
Employees who have completed at least 80 working days in the preceding 12 months, where required by local law.
Leave Entitlement

Eligible employees are entitled to:

Up to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two children.
Up to 12 weeks of paid maternity leave for subsequent children.
Additional unpaid leave may be approved based on medical recommendations and business requirements.
Adoption Leave

Employees legally adopting a child below the age of one year may be eligible for:

12 weeks of paid adoption leave.
Medical Documentation

Employees must provide:

Medical certificates confirming pregnancy.
Expected delivery date documentation.
Any additional supporting documents requested by HR.
Job Protection

Employees returning from maternity leave will:

Return to the same role whenever reasonably possible.
Be offered an equivalent position if organizational changes have occurred.
Flexible Return Options

Subject to managerial approval, employees may request:

Reduced work schedules.
Temporary remote work arrangements.
Phased return-to-work plans.
3. Work From Home Policy
Purpose

The Work From Home (WFH) Policy establishes guidelines for employees performing work remotely while maintaining productivity, collaboration, and data security.

Eligibility

WFH eligibility depends on:

Job responsibilities.
Business requirements.
Performance history.
Manager approval.

Not all positions are eligible for remote work.

Standard Remote Work Arrangement

Employees approved for WFH may:

Work remotely up to 3 days per week.
Follow standard working hours unless alternative arrangements have been approved.
Equipment

The company may provide:

Laptop computers.
Monitors.
Headsets.
Security software.

Employees are responsible for maintaining company equipment in good condition.

Security Requirements

Employees working remotely must:

Use company-approved VPN connections.
Enable multi-factor authentication.
Avoid accessing company systems through public Wi-Fi without approved security measures.
Performance Expectations

Remote employees are expected to:

Attend scheduled meetings.
Remain reachable during working hours.
Meet all productivity and quality expectations.
Revocation of WFH Privileges

WFH arrangements may be modified or revoked due to:

Business needs.
Security concerns.
Performance issues.
Policy violations.
4. Health Insurance Policy
Purpose

TBHSI provides health insurance coverage to support employee wellness and reduce financial burdens associated with medical care.

Eligibility

The health insurance plan covers:

Full-time employees.
Eligible dependents as defined by the insurance provider.

Coverage begins on the first day of the month following employee onboarding.

Coverage Details

The standard health plan includes:

Hospitalization

Coverage for:

Inpatient treatment.
Surgical procedures.
Emergency care.
Intensive care services.
Outpatient Care

Coverage may include:

Doctor consultations.
Diagnostic testing.
Specialist referrals.
Preventive Care

Eligible preventive services include:

Annual health checkups.
Vaccinations.
Health screenings.
Dependents

Employees may enroll:

Spouses.
Dependent children.
Parents, where applicable under company plans.

Additional premiums may apply.

Claims Process

Employees must:

Submit claims according to insurer requirements.
Provide supporting documentation.
Adhere to claim submission deadlines.
Exclusions

Coverage may not include:

Cosmetic procedures.
Experimental treatments.
Non-medically necessary services.
Conditions excluded by the insurance provider.
Wellness Programs

The company may periodically offer:

Mental health support programs.
Fitness initiatives.
Health awareness campaigns.
Employee assistance services.
5. Travel Reimbursement Policy
Purpose

This policy ensures employees are reimbursed for reasonable business-related travel expenses incurred while conducting company business.

Eligibility

Travel reimbursement applies to:

Approved business trips.
Client visits.
Conferences.
Training programs.
Internal meetings requiring travel.
Pre-Approval Requirement

All travel must receive:

Manager approval.
Budget confirmation when applicable.

Unauthorized travel expenses may be rejected.

Air Travel
Domestic Travel

Employees should generally book:

Economy class tickets.
International Travel

The following may be approved:

Economy class for flights under 6 hours.
Premium economy or business class for eligible senior employees and long-haul travel, subject to approval.
Accommodation

Employees should select reasonably priced accommodations aligned with company travel standards.

Luxury accommodations require prior approval.

Ground Transportation

Reimbursable expenses include:

Taxis.
Ride-sharing services.
Airport transfers.
Public transportation.

Employees should choose cost-effective transportation options whenever practical.

Meals

Meal reimbursement limits are:

Domestic travel: ₹2,000 per day.
International travel: Based on destination-specific allowances.

Alcoholic beverages are generally not reimbursable.

Expense Submission

Employees must:

Submit expense reports within 15 days of trip completion.
Attach receipts for all eligible expenses.
Provide business justification where requested.
Non-Reimbursable Expenses

Examples include:

Personal entertainment.
Traffic fines.
Personal shopping.
Family member expenses.
Unapproved upgrades.
6. Notice Period Policy
Purpose

The Notice Period Policy facilitates smooth employee transitions while minimizing operational disruption.

Employee Resignation

Employees intending to resign must submit:

A written resignation letter through the HRMS system.
Standard Notice Period

Unless otherwise specified in the employment agreement:

Employee Category	Notice Period
Associate	30 Days
Senior Associate	60 Days
Manager	90 Days
Senior Manager and Above	90 Days
Notice Buyout

Employees may request partial or full notice period buyout.

Approval depends on:

Project commitments.
Knowledge transfer status.
Manager and HR approval.

The company is not obligated to approve buyout requests.

Company-Initiated Separation

Notice periods for company-initiated separation will be governed by:

Employment contracts.
Applicable labor laws.
Internal disciplinary procedures.
Knowledge Transfer

Employees serving notice are expected to:

Complete documentation.
Transfer responsibilities.
Support replacement onboarding.
Return company assets.
Final Settlement

Final payments may include:

Salary up to last working day.
Approved reimbursements.
Leave encashment where applicable.

Settlement timelines will follow applicable regulations and company procedures.

7. Training and Development Policy
Purpose

TBHSI believes continuous learning is essential for individual growth and organizational success.

Scope

This policy covers:

Technical training.
Leadership development.
Compliance training.
Professional certifications.
External workshops.
Mandatory Training

Employees must complete assigned mandatory training programs within specified deadlines.

Examples include:

Information security awareness.
Data privacy compliance.
Workplace ethics.
Anti-harassment training.

Failure to complete mandatory training may affect performance evaluations.

Professional Development

Employees may request participation in:

Industry conferences.
Certification programs.
Specialized workshops.
Online learning platforms.
Training Approval Process

Requests should include:

Training objectives.
Estimated costs.
Business relevance.
Expected outcomes.

Approvals require:

Manager endorsement.
Budget availability.
HR review where applicable.
Training Sponsorship

The company may fully or partially sponsor:

Certification fees.
Course fees.
Examination costs.
Training materials.
Service Commitment

For company-sponsored programs exceeding ₹50,000, employees may be required to sign a service agreement requiring continued employment for a specified period after training completion.

Knowledge Sharing

Employees attending external training programs may be required to:

Share learning outcomes.
Conduct internal workshops.
Create training materials for team use.
Learning Platforms

The company may provide access to:

E-learning portals.
Technical learning libraries.
Professional development resources.
Internal learning management systems.
Training Records

HR maintains records of:

Completed courses.
Certifications.
Learning achievements.
Compliance training status.

These records may be considered during:

Performance reviews.
Promotion decisions.
Succession planning.
Policy Amendments

TBHSI reserves the right to modify, amend, suspend, or terminate any policy described in this handbook at any time based on business requirements, legal obligations, or organizational priorities.

Employees will be notified of significant policy changes through official communication channels.
